About me

I'm currently studying for a diploma in Automotive Engineering at Pc Kinyanjui TTI in Nairobi, where I'm building a practical foundation in engine systems, electrical circuits, and vehicle diagnostics. Alongside that, I work as a motorbike rider with Bolt — time on the road that's taught me a lot about how vehicles actually behave and fail, day to day, not just in theory.

Outside the classroom, I've taught myself web design and basic development, and I use both AutoCAD and diagnostic tools like OBD scanners to explore how automotive systems are built and repaired. I also create content around automotive topics and technology, and I'm building Masitsa Motors, a car dealership concept site, as a way to bring the engineering and development sides of what I do together.

I don't have a long résumé yet — what I have is a habit of finishing what I start, and a genuine interest in how things work, whether that's a carburetor or a codebase.

Case study

Masitsa Motors

Visit the site ↗
Problem

A small car dealership needs a way to showcase vehicles and take enquiries online, without the cost or complexity of a full booking-and-payments backend.

Solution

A fast, static multi-page site — home, inventory, and a sell-your-car page — where every "Enquire" button opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to the dealership's number, turning browsing into a direct conversation.

Technology

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and deployed on GitHub Pages, with the WhatsApp Click-to-Chat links handling enquiries instead of a database or server.

Currently working on

Continuing to expand the inventory pages and refine the sell-your-car flow as the dealership concept grows.
